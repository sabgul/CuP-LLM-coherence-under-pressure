================================================================================
SECONDARY: CONDITION DISCRIMINATION (ROC AUC)
================================================================================

This measures how well each judge's session-mean coherence separates
the honest and scheming PROMPT CONDITIONS. It does not measure detection
of genuine deception — only discrimination between experimental conditions.

Classifier: lower coherence -> predict scheming (score = -coherence).
Unit: one session mean per observation.
Bootstrap: 10k resamples of sessions within subject x scenario x condition.
